## Emphasis

*This text will be italic*  

**This text will be bold**  

*You **can** combine them*

## Lists

### Unordered

* Item 1
* Item 2
* Item 2a
* Item 2b
    * Item 3a
    * Item 3b

### Ordered

1. Item 1
2. Item 2
3. Item 3
    1. Item 3a
    2. Item 3b
